## Shipped

- **Notation**: neutral white square, the method's words written short: `D` dry, `S` sticky, `T` tacky, `C` creamy,
  `EW` egg white, `W` watery. Justisse's own stamp key is in its paid user guide (`stampNote`).
- **Peak**: last day of peak-type mucus; egg white and watery are peak-type (`peak`, `observations[4]`, `[5]`, hds).
- **Count**: 3 days after the peak (`fertileAfterOvulation.afterPeak`, hds: "Count of 3").
- The bleeding items are not consumed: hosts show them in their own row.
